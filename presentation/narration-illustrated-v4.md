# DivX narration · illustrated v4

22 September 2026. Companion to the revised nine-slide deck.


**1 — DivX**

DivX lets you keep the stock exposure and sell the right to a year of dividends. A dividend is a payment a company makes to its shareholders. We built this for Stocklana on Solana. The program and demo work today with test assets.

**2 — Two sides of one trade**

People already trade dividends separately in traditional finance. Banks use dividend contracts to manage risk from structured products. Investment funds buy exposure to future dividends. With DivX, a stockholder retains PT for stock exposure and sells the annual DR to a buyer for payment.

**3 — One deposit, two tokens**

In this example, deposit 100 stock tokens into a vault. Receive 100 PT for the stock exposure and 100 DR for the year’s dividend rights. The stock tokens stay in the vault. You can keep, transfer or trade each side. The counts describe claims, not a promised dividend amount.

**4 — An established idea**

More than 21 million dividend contracts traded on Eurex in 2024. In crypto, Pendle separates principal from future yield. These markets show that people want to trade income separately from an asset. DivX brings that idea to stock tokens.

**5 — One year of dividend rights**

Each stock token and year has its own series. Deposit and split before the year starts; issuance closes at the cutoff. During the year, people can trade existing PT or DR, and DR accumulates qualified dividends. A midyear buyer gets DR from another holder, not from new issuance. Matching PT and DR can recombine while the series is active. After finalization, each side can redeem separately, and accrued rights do not expire.

**6 — Why Solana**

Stock-token trading is already happening on Solana. Galaxy reports that its share reached over 95 percent during the second quarter of 2026. We want to give existing capital more uses and attract additional capital to Solana through DivX. Fast settlement, fractional positions and auditable records can make both tokens useful to other builders.

**7 — Both sides are composable**

Raydium and Meteora offer liquidity markets for compatible tokens. We have demonstrated a dividend-right market using Raydium with test assets. Compatible markets could use Jupiter for routing and orders. Streamflow could support agreed-price sales. Jupiter Lock and Streamflow could schedule delivery, while Squads could manage a shared treasury. Lending comes later, with reliable prices and enough liquidity.

**8 — A working guided demo**

The guided tour at https://dividendx.payai.network/demos/ lets anyone split a stock token, put it back together, see the dividend effect and trade dividend rights with a second wallet. It runs real program transactions in a private sandbox with synthetic assets and accelerated time. The public devnet app uses the real calendar. Our test catalog represents selected stocks from Ondo, Backpack and xStocks.

More liquidity, rolling dividend strategies and other networks come later. Try the guided demo at dividendx.payai.network. No wallet or real funds are needed.
