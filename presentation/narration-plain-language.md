# DividendX narration

Approximate running time: 3 minutes. Spoken word count: 400.

**1 — DividendX**  
DividendX lets you stay invested in a stock and sell the right to a future dividend. A dividend is a payment a company makes to its shareholders. Sell that claim, and you get paid today. The buyer gets the right to that dividend.

**2 — Two sides of one trade**  
People already trade dividends separately from stocks in traditional finance. Banks sell contracts tied to future dividends to reduce risk. Investment funds such as Survista buy them at a discount. DividendX brings that established market to people who hold stock tokens.

**3 — One deposit, two claims**  
Deposit a stock token into our vault. The vault creates two tokens: PT for the stock position and DR for the dividend. Keep the stock side and sell the dividend side. Each can be exchanged for its share of the stock tokens held in the vault. Their dollar value can change.

**4 — Established markets, familiar behavior**  
More than 21 million dividend contracts traded on Eurex, a major exchange, in 2024. In crypto, Pendle lets people trade the future income from their tokens. It reported an average of about 96 million dollars traded per day that year.

**5 — Tokenized stocks make this possible**  
Companies paid 1.75 trillion dollars in dividends in 2024. Tokenized stocks now bring stock investments into crypto wallets. Names like Apple, Microsoft and Coca-Cola already have tokens on Solana. That gives us the assets to build this dividend market onchain.

**6 — One workflow across issuers**  
Solana lets trades settle quickly. The buyer gets the dividend claim and the seller gets paid in one transaction. Holders use the same flow for supported tokens from Backpack, xStocks and Ondo. Each stock token has its own vault.

**7 — Small positions, public records**  
You can buy or sell a small part of a dividend claim. Anyone can check the vault's holdings and see which claims have been paid out. Other Solana apps can use these claims too, so wallets and trading apps can build on the same market.

**8 — Coca-Cola, in dollars**  
Take Coca-Cola. Our calculator uses a real dividend event for KOx, its xStocks token. For tokens representing 100 shares before the dividend, it shows about 8,935 dollars of stock value and 37 dollars and 10 cents in reinvested dividends. Those values use a price calculated from the historical event. The calculator works today. Next comes the vault.

**9 — Solana issuers first**  
For the hackathon, we're building the full trade: deposit, split, sell the dividend, then redeem each side separately. We start with selected stocks from Backpack, xStocks and Ondo on Solana. More networks come later, with Solana as the home for dividend trading.
