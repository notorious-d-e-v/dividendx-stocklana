# Submission description draft

DivX is a Solana vault design that separates a tokenized stock position into stock exposure and one event's dividend rights. A holder keeps the stock exposure while offering the dividend claim to a buyer. Both claims remain backed by the same locked xStock collateral and redeem in xStock units, so their dollar value can change.

Traditional markets already have both sides. Bank trading desks hedge or recycle dividend risk from structured products, while asset managers and hedge funds trade expected dividend payouts. Eurex documents asset manager Survista buying discounted single-stock dividend futures. DivX applies that familiar market structure to physically collateralized xStock claims without suggesting those institutions use the product.

The current prototype includes an approved product interface and a working historical calculator. It uses Coca-Cola, ticker KO, represented on Solana as KOx. For 100 pre-event share-equivalents, the 15 September 2026 event allocates about $8,935 of stock exposure and $37.10 of reinvested dividends at the event-implied $89.35 reference price. The underlying outputs remain 100.0000 KOx and 0.4152 KOx. The price comes from the issuer's net cashflow and multiplier ratio; it is not a market quote, sale price or guaranteed cash payout. The calculator uses a dated source snapshot and does not claim a vault captured the historical event.

The hackathon build target is a complete test-asset flow: deposit, issue paired PT and DR claims, sell the dividend claim using a controlled counterparty, process the verified historical event, and redeem each side separately. Claims are divisible. Onchain state can expose vault balances, outstanding claims and redemption history, while issuer data and code correctness remain separate trust and verification concerns. The accounting checks event classification against the token multiplier so a stock split cannot become dividend yield.

The roadmap moves from Coca-Cola to Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA xStocks, then selected Backpack and Ondo assets on Solana. Longer-term research covers Coinbase assets on Base and the new wallet-held Robinhood Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain, with Solana as the intended home base for dividend liquidity. No deployed mainnet vault, production liquidity, partnership, automatic bridge route or guaranteed return is claimed in this draft.
