# Submission description draft

DivX is a shared dividend-market design for tokenized stocks on Solana. A stock holder keeps the stock exposure while offering one event's dividend rights to a buyer. A deposit creates paired PT and DR claims backed by that stock token. Every series keeps one issuer, mint and event in its own vault, and both claims redeem in the deposited token, so their dollar value can change.

Traditional markets already have both sides. Bank trading desks hedge or recycle dividend risk from structured products, while asset managers and hedge funds trade expected dividend payouts. Eurex documents asset manager Survista buying discounted single-stock dividend futures. DivX applies that familiar market structure to physically collateralized xStock claims without suggesting those institutions use the product.

The initial integration scope covers xStocks, Backpack and Ondo. Observed tokens in all three families use Token-2022 Scaled UI Amount, allowing one allocation engine for qualified reinvested-dividend events. Each issuer still requires its own event reader, mint and authority checks, and custody policy. Tokens with missing event data, restricted ownership, no dividend rights, incompatible transfer fees or other unsupported controls cannot open a series automatically. The shared interface does not pool issuer collateral or make claims from different issuers fungible.

The current prototype includes an approved product interface and a working historical calculator. Its source-verified reference is Coca-Cola, ticker KO, represented on Solana as KOx. For 100 pre-event share-equivalents, the 15 September 2026 event allocates about $8,935 of stock exposure and $37.10 of reinvested dividends at the event-implied $89.35 reference price. The underlying outputs remain 100.0000 KOx and 0.4152 KOx. The price comes from the issuer's net cashflow and multiplier ratio; it is not a market quote, sale price or guaranteed cash payout. No Backpack or Ondo dividend event is fabricated to imply completed settlement support.

The hackathon build target is one complete workflow across supported Solana stock tokens: discover and qualify an asset, deposit, issue paired PT and DR claims, sell the dividend claim using a controlled counterparty, and redeem each side separately. Claims are divisible. Onchain state can expose vault balances, outstanding claims and redemption history, while issuer reserves, event data and code correctness remain separate trust and verification concerns. The KOx replay remains the complete reference transaction until other issuers provide source data that supports an equivalent replay.

The roadmap begins with qualified assets from xStocks, Backpack and Ondo, adds more eligible Solana stocks after dividend and custody checks, and considers Coinbase assets on Base and the new wallet-held Robinhood Stock Tokens later. Solana remains the intended home base for dividend liquidity. No deployed mainnet vault, completed multi-issuer integration, production liquidity, issuer partnership, automatic bridge route or guaranteed return is claimed in this draft.
