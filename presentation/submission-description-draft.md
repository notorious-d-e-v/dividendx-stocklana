# Submission description draft

DividendX is a permissionless dividend-market design for selected stock tokens on Solana. A stock holder keeps the stock exposure while offering one event's dividend rights to a buyer. A deposit creates paired PT and DR claims backed by that stock token. Every series keeps one issuer, mint and event in its own vault, and both claims redeem in the deposited token, so their dollar value can change.

Traditional markets already have both sides. Bank trading desks hedge or recycle dividend risk from structured products, while asset managers and hedge funds trade expected dividend payouts. Eurex documents asset manager Survista buying discounted single-stock dividend futures. DividendX applies that familiar market structure to claims backed by stock tokens without suggesting those institutions use the product.

The initial candidate package covers 15 mints across six companies: Coca-Cola, Apple, Microsoft, Micron, Nike and IBM. xStocks and Ondo each contribute six candidates; Backpack contributes Micron, Nike and IBM. These are candidates, not completed integrations, and the six companies are not all available from every issuer. Observed tokens in all three families use Token-2022 Scaled UI Amount, allowing one allocation engine for qualified reinvested-dividend events. Each issuer still requires its own event reader, mint and authority checks, and custody policy. Permissionless means DividendX does not add a holder allowlist or issuer-specific approved-vault onboarding for admitted assets; issuer controls and product restrictions remain. Permissioned, no-dividend and fee-bearing products are outside the build scope.

The current prototype includes an approved product interface and a working historical calculator. Its source-verified reference is Coca-Cola, ticker KO, represented on Solana as KOx. For 100 pre-event share-equivalents, the 15 September 2026 event allocates about $8,935 of stock exposure and $37.10 of reinvested dividends at the event-implied $89.35 reference price. The underlying outputs remain 100.0000 KOx and 0.4152 KOx. The price comes from the issuer's net cashflow and multiplier ratio; it is not a market quote, sale price or guaranteed cash payout. No Backpack or Ondo dividend event is fabricated to imply completed settlement support.

The hackathon build target is one complete workflow across supported Solana stock tokens: discover and qualify an asset, deposit, issue paired PT and DR claims, sell the dividend claim using a controlled counterparty, and redeem each side separately. Claims are divisible. Onchain state can expose vault balances, outstanding claims and redemption history, while issuer reserves, event data and code correctness remain separate trust and verification concerns. KOx has a complete source-verified replay fixture. Backpack MU has a fully bracketed sourced onchain reconstruction, while its official event ledger and finality semantics remain unverified. An Ondo event still needs authenticated source binding. All vault execution remains planned.

The roadmap starts with selected stocks from xStocks, Backpack and Ondo, proving one dividend event per issuer before expanding within the package. Coinbase assets on Base and wallet-held Robinhood Stock Tokens come later. Solana remains the intended home base for dividend liquidity. No deployed mainnet vault, completed multi-issuer integration, production liquidity, issuer partnership, automatic bridge route or guaranteed return is claimed in this draft.
