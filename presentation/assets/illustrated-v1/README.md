# DivX illustrations — v1

Original artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool for the separate illustrated pitch deck. Both files are transparent 1254 × 1254 PNGs and can be reused in the frontend.

- [Stock and dividend coupon](stock-dividend-coupon.png): slide 3, a visual metaphor for separating dividend rights from stock exposure. Its proportions do not represent financial values.
- [Claim composability](claim-composability.png): slide 7, a dividend claim connecting a wallet, public records and a trading app. These are conceptual illustrations, not product screenshots or completed integrations.

The editable labels and mechanism diagrams remain in the presentation. The illustrations themselves are raster images.
