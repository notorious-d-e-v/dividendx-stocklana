# DivX narration

Approximate running time: 3 minutes. Word count: 380.

**1 — DivX**<br>
DivX lets you stay invested in a stock and sell the right to a future dividend. A dividend is a payment a company makes to its shareholders. Sell that claim, and you get paid today. In exchange, the buyer gets the right to that dividend.

**2 — Two sides of one trade**  
People already trade dividends separately from stocks in traditional finance. Banks sell contracts tied to future dividends to reduce risk. Investment funds such as Survista buy them at a discount. DivX brings that established market to people who hold tokenized stocks.

**3 — One deposit, two claims**  
Deposit a stock token into our vault. The vault creates two tokens: PT for the stock position and DR for the dividend. Keep the stock side and sell the dividend side. Each can be later exchanged for its share of the stock tokens held in the vault.

**4 — Established markets, familiar behavior**  
In traditional finance, more than 21 million dividend contracts traded on Eurex in 2024. In crypto, Pendle lets people trade the future income from their tokens (yield). Pendle reported an average of about 96 million dollars traded per day that year.

**5 — Tokenized stocks make this possible**  
Companies paid 1.75 trillion dollars in dividends in 2024. Tokenized stocks now bring stock investments into crypto wallets. Names like Apple, Microsoft and Coca-Cola already have tokens on Solana. That gives us the assets to build this dividend market onchain.

**6 — One workflow across issuers**  
Solana lets trades settle quickly. The buyer gets the dividend claim and the seller gets paid in one transaction. We're building for tokenized stocks from Backpack, xStocks and Ondo.

**7 — Small positions, public records**  
You can even buy or sell a tiny (fractional) part of a dividend claim. Anyone can check the vault's holdings and see which claims have been paid out. Other Solana apps can use these claims too, so wallets and trading apps can build on the same market.

**8 — Coca-Cola, in dollars**  
Take Coca-Cola. Our calculator uses a real dividend event for KOx, its xStocks token. For KOx tokens representing 100 shares before the dividend, it shows about 8,935 dollars of stock value and 37.10 dollars in reinvested dividends. Those two separate assets can be built upon.

**9 — Solana issuers first**  
For the hackathon, we're building the full trade: deposit, split, sell the dividend, then redeem each side separately. We start with selected stocks from Backpack, xStocks and Ondo on Solana. More networks come later, with Solana as the home for dividend trading.
