01 · Idea

Financial editorial art with one job.

Each image makes an ownership concept easier to grasp. The system is warm and tactile, but its visual charm never adds a claim the product cannot support.

01

Make the entitlement legible.

One idea reads before texture or decoration.

02

Use tactile objects sparingly.

Printed matter gives abstract rights a familiar physical vocabulary.

03

Let labels carry exact meaning.

Color reinforces names. It never replaces values, states or issuer identity.

04

Leave room to think.

Bold silhouettes and negative space keep the art calm beside type.

02 · Decision

Choose the visual by its responsibility.

When a visual must be both evocative and exact, split the work: illustration introduces the concept; native components explain the mechanism.

◇

Illustration

Use for a single conceptual metaphor: separation, custody identity, divisibility, or composability. It can orient and invite, but not prove.

↗

Functional icon

Use to identify an action or control at small size. Replace a complex scene with an icon at 64 px; never crush the whole PNG into a glyph.

≡

Editable diagram

Use native text and vectors for exact order, values, ratios, labels, accounting or transaction paths. Raster art is not a financial chart.

03 · Construction

Sturdy planes. Quiet ink. A visible gap.

The recipe is an optical starting point for new work. It does not pretend to measure or retroactively standardize the existing PNGs.

Shape
Softened paper rectangles, clipped or perforated edges, shallow overlaps.
Line
At 320 px optical width: 2–3 px outer ink and 1–1.5 px internal rules.
Texture
Low-contrast paper grain inside objects only. Never across the canvas.
View
Front or gentle three-quarter view with 5–12° tilt and minimal shadow.
Objects
One to three for a concept; no more than four for a scene.
Detail
One or two internal cues. No micro-detail required for recognition.

These are nominal optical targets for future artwork, not measurements of current raster files.

06 · Semantics

Consistent objects, consistent meanings.

Metaphors stay useful only when their meaning is stable. Decorative details never upgrade an illustration into evidence, audit, custody or integration.

Certificate

The original stock token before deposit, or Stock exposure (PT) after the split; nearby native copy must distinguish them.

Coupon

One event’s Dividend rights entitlement, not guaranteed cash or a recurring stream.

Gap

Separable ownership, not withdrawal, failure or a broken transaction.

Custody tray

Collateral identity. Multiple trays remain distinct with no shared pool.

Fractions

Divisibility only. Their area and count do not represent exact allocation.

Wallet / ledger / app

Conceptual touchpoints, not a claim of completed integration.

08 · Usage

Editorial presence, product restraint.

A pitch may use roughly two illustrations across nine slides as a pacing example, not a fixed law. Product states should remain direct and factual.

ContextUseRestraint
PitchConcept or scene beside a short claim.Do not let art compete with the key statement.
OnboardingOne concept image followed by native explanation.Do not imply unavailable wallet, custody or trading support.
ExplanationIllustration introduces the model; editable diagram handles mechanics.Never encode ratios in illustrated object size.
Empty stateSmall spot only when it clarifies the absence.Pending, error and rejection need status components and text.
MotionOptional one-shot fade and 4–8 px settle over 200–320 ms.No loops, fake transaction progress or increasing balance; respect reduced motion.

09 · Production

Files, prompts and handoff.

Generated PNGs are canonical raster sources, not editable vectors. Layered motion requires deliberate manual vector artwork.

Files and delivery

Name files by subject and version. Preserve a genuine transparent PNG, then derive WebP or AVIF for product delivery. Proposed budgets are 80–180 KB for a spot and no more than 350 KB for a scene; existing source PNGs may exceed them.

Do not bake a checkerboard, wrap a bitmap in SVG, auto-trace paper grain, or claim generated elements are editable layers. Record prompt, dimensions, byte size, checksum, status and review notes.

Prompt and accessibility

Start with the reusable style brief, then state audience, metaphor, concepts, forbidden implications, tier, size, background and attached reference assets. Token colors are targets; raster generation may only approximate them.

Alt text describes the contributed idea. Use an empty alt when adjacent copy already supplies it. Keep exact values, labels and flows native; palette alone does not prove accessible contrast.
