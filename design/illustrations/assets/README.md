## New studies for this guide

- [Separate issuer custody](issuer-separated-custody-v1.png): three distinct trays illustrate separate backing. Use a broad placement; simplify ornamental certificate borders for a compact derivative.
- [Fractional dividend claim](fractional-dividend-v1.png): two amber paper pieces suggest divisibility. Their areas do not represent financial values. Use this raster at 128 px or larger; choose a simpler icon at 64 px.
- [Both sides are composable](stock-and-dividend-composability-v1.png): Stock exposure (PT) and Dividend rights (DR) can each be used by compatible wallets and trading apps after the split. This is a conceptual compatibility scene, not evidence of live integrations. The underlying stock remains locked as backing and is not freely reused or double pledged. See the [source prompt](../prompts/stock-and-dividend-composability-v1.md).

The studies were created with the built-in image generation tool and reviewed as conceptual artwork, not evidence of live integrations. The original transparent PNGs are raster masters; production compression and layered vector redraws are separate steps.
