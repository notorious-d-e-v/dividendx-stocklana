# Final extension prompts — v1

Method: built-in image generation, new asset generation. These final files were generated from text after reference-based attempts and a background-extraction edit returned RGB images with baked checkerboards. Rejected attempts were not added to the library. Both final PNGs were inspected and their real alpha channels verified. The two existing deck illustrations remain the style anchors.

## issuer-separated-custody-v1

Generate a new original editorial illustration on a transparent background: three small blue open document trays, physically separate in a horizontal row with wide empty gaps. Each tray contains its own one ivory stock certificate with a blue border and small amber coupon. No shared base or container, no connections. Simple flat hand-inked contours, subtly textured screenprint fills, gentle shallow perspective, matching the visual language of paper certificates and tickets. Palette ink #101820, paper #F6F3EC, blue #2457F5, amber #C46A00. Certificates have only two plain rules, no text, seals or symbols. Large clean silhouettes, economical detail, no gradients, no 3D shine, no shadows. Centered landscape illustration with moderate clear margins, complete objects. This is a brand illustration, not a chart. Output as isolated cutout with actual transparency and alpha, with empty transparent space around and between trays.

## fractional-dividend-v1

Generate a new original compact editorial spot illustration on a transparent background: a single amber perforated coupon ticket separated into two unequal pieces. A small rightmost segment is gently pulled away from the longer left section, leaving a clear transparent gap. The two pieces clearly belong to the same ticket. One simple amber border, one neutral short line inside the large piece, no other detail. Ink #101820 slightly hand-drawn crisp outlines, amber #C46A00 with warm pale amber #FFF0D2 paper, almost flat front view tilted 5 degrees, extremely subtle paper texture. Bold simple silhouette readable when small. No words, numbers, icons, foliage, coins, currency marks, gradients, shadows, or surroundings. Centered single compact group in a square frame with modest breathing room. This is a conceptual divisibility metaphor, not a data chart. Output isolated cutout with real alpha transparency around and between the two paper pieces.
