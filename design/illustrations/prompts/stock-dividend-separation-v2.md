# Stock and annual dividend rights — hero study v2

Built-in image-generation tool; local review variant. Existing illustration assets and the earlier editable hero diagram are preserved.

## Prompt

Create a new hero variant of the first reference illustration, using the second reference only for its restrained tactile paper and ink style. Original DividendX financial editorial illustration in the “Paper instruments, digital ownership” system. Tactile printed matter; crisp slightly imperfect dark ink outlines; flat fills; low-contrast paper grain inside objects only; gentle front or three-quarter perspective; generous negative space. Canonical ink #101820, cobalt #2457F5, amber #C46A00, warm paper #FFFEFB, soft blue #E9EEFF and soft amber #FFF0D2. No readable text, logos, numbers, performance imagery, coins, characters, glossy 3D, gradients, official seals or photorealism. Genuine transparent background, not a painted checkerboard.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Audience / placement: a clear, friendly stock-token app hero for non-specialists, artwork displayed at approximately 400 by 280 pixels beside the headline “One stock. Two tokens.” Native UI supplies labels; bake no words into the picture.
Metaphor and single concept: two independently held rights, made tangible as a blue stock-exposure certificate and a separated amber annual dividend-rights slip. The amber slip represents rights within one selected term, not guaranteed cash or a promised count of dividend payments.
Primary objects: exactly two printed paper objects. A sturdy landscape blue-bordered certificate on the left, tilted gently counterclockwise, and a narrower amber paper claim on the right, tilted slightly clockwise. Match their adjacent softly perforated edges but leave a clearly visible gap; make both silhouettes substantial and readable. Three restrained ink rules on each object stand in for printed matter. No wax seal, checkmark, crest, signature or micro-ornament.
Composition: compact horizontal group, slight offset in vertical position, light paper faces, very shallow depth and minimal shadow. Keep the artwork generous within the canvas, with 10–12% clear space. No arrows, connector lines, wallets, computers, background texture, backdrop or extra objects. Do not encode any numerical allocation in the object sizes.
Tier: concept, 240–420px optical width; wider-than-tall canvas, safe on warm cream or white.
Reference 1: existing stock-dividend-coupon.png, borrow the blue-certificate/amber-detachable-right metaphor and sturdy outlines; simplify ornate details and remove its decorative seal.
Reference 2: stock-and-dividend-composability-v1.png, borrow tactile paper and the restrained ink style only, not its app/wallet/connector composition.
Output: true transparent PNG; no typography, no logos, no watermark.

## Targeted refinement

A second built-in edit requested removal of the broad soft outer shadow/glow, preserving paper shapes, blue/amber colors, internal grain, perforated edges, rules and ink contours, with no extra elements. The delivered RGBA file has zero alpha in the surrounding canvas; final placement is checked on the warm app background rather than judging transparent pixels from their hidden RGB values.
