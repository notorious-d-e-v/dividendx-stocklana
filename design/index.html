Design premise

DivX turns a single collateral position into two legible entitlements. The split itself is the interface, the identity, and the explanation.

Financial, with a pulse.

Warm surfaces lower the glare. Ink does the reading work. Blue and amber only appear when they communicate who owns which part of the collateral.
