## Product distinctions

- **Brokerage entitlement:** Backpack describes ordinary U.S. stock holdings as UCC Article 8 security entitlements with cash dividends and corporate actions processed through brokerage infrastructure. See [Introducing Backpack Securities](https://learn.backpack.exchange/blog/introducing-backpack-securities) and [stocks/about](https://backpack.exchange/stocks/about). A ticker appearing in `GET /api/v1/securities` proves brokerage tradability, not onchain withdrawal.
- **Backpack-issued token:** MU/SKHY/SPCX announcements describe a separate, self-custodied Solana token redeemable into the corresponding Backpack security entitlement; token dividends are reinvested and reflected by proportional token-balance economics. Availability must be checked independently through the per-token flags in `GET /api/v1/assets`.
- **Superstate Opening Bell:** Backpack's [Superstate announcement](https://learn.backpack.exchange/blog/superstate-tokenized-equities-onchain) says Backpack is a venue for equities **issued through Superstate's Opening Bell**, not their issuer. Superstate says these are companies' registered shares maintained by an SEC-registered transfer agent, with allowlisted wallets and issuer-specific networks; see [Opening Bell](https://www.superstate.com/opening-bell) and its [investor docs](https://docs.superstate.com/tokenized-equities/investors). This is a different legal/technical product from Backpack-issued entitlement tokens.

## Corporate-action data availability

- Backpack publishes descriptive stock dividend pages such as [AAPL dividends](https://backpack.exchange/stocks/AAPL/dividends) and [MU dividends](https://backpack.exchange/stocks/MU/dividends), plus a general [corporate-actions explainer](https://learn.backpack.exchange/articles/what-are-corporate-actions). These are reference/UI surfaces, not a documented Backpack-issued-token event ledger.
- The public API documents stock discovery, sessions, prices, markets, and account history, but no public dividend/corporate-action history endpoint or scaled-multiplier event feed was found. The onchain multiplier and transaction history are observable, but a reliable dividend/corporate-action classification source and record/ex/payable metadata remain unverified.
- Integration implication: MU proves the accounting primitive can be reused, but a production reader should be added only with an approved mint list, current deposit/withdraw flags, expected authorities/extensions, active-vs-pending multiplier/time handling, and a trustworthy event feed. No broad claim that every Backpack brokerage ticker is tokenizable is supported.

## RPC method used

`POST https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com` with `getAccountInfo`, `encoding: jsonParsed`, `commitment: finalized`. Values above were observed directly from public mainnet at the stated slots; Backpack's official article/API supplied the mint identity.
