# Mainnet readiness and hackathon claim boundary — 20 September 2026

**Verdict: DividendX is a working annual-claims prototype and public synthetic-devnet demonstration, not a live issuer-stock or mainnet settlement service.** The shared program and wallet can execute the custody and claim mechanics. No selected issuer has passed the separate live custody and annual-event qualification gates. No real-asset transaction, mainnet deployment, production audit, issuer agreement, or qualified issuer settlement is evidenced or authorized. The hackathon presentation should show the working flow and say these gaps plainly.

## Gates to real issuer custody and settlement

1. **Live custody admission (issuer/operations, then engineering).** For each *exact* issuer/mint/year, verify current Token-2022 profile and authority controls, token terms and ordinary PDA vault transferability; demonstrate actual permitted deposit, transfer, paired exit and deficit behavior with a properly authorized holder under a separately approved, bounded test. The fifteen-mint snapshot proves local instruction compatibility only. Depending on the exact mint profile, issuer-controlled freeze, pause, permanent-delegate or upgrade powers can impair vault transfers. An asset needing special vault approval falls outside the current ordinary-custody scope. Record ongoing change detection and holder disclosure, not a one-time mint snapshot. [Custody review](issuer-custody-review.md), [custody conformance contract](../spec/issuer-custody-conformance-v1.md), [program trust contract](../spec/program-v1.md).
2. **Settlement-grade source policy (issuer/external evidence).** Obtain authoritative official reference-market civil ex-dates; exact event-to-mint and isolated M0/M1 factor joins; ordinary dividend versus split, maintenance, cancellation or unsupported-action classification; stable revisions; complete historical coverage; late-payment/correction behavior; and a defensible finality/dispute policy. The observed xStocks `Initial` state and timing mismatches are unresolved; Backpack's reconstructed MU transaction lacks a full action ledger; Ondo multiplier history can omit actions and is not a classified event ledger. A current factor, paginated-to-end response, year-end date or operator checkbox cannot prove annual completeness. Issuer-specific answers are drafted but have not been sent. [Public policy check](research/issuer-public-policy-check-2026-09-18.md), [data requests](issuer-data-requests.md), [qualification review](issuer-qualification-review.md).
3. **Reviewed attestor-to-program path (engineering and operations).** Only after a source policy passes should a versioned, reproducible qualification decision produce an EventInput and complete-period attestation. Bind evidence digest, issuer/mint/year identity, revisions, official dates, factor bits, coverage window and policy version to each signed action; independently review and replay it before any finalization. Define key custody, separation of duties, monitoring, correction escalation and an irreversible post-finalization dispute path. The current server readers deliberately have no settlement writer and every dossier remains unsigned. The chain verifies signatures and arithmetic, **not** the truth or completeness of issuer data. [Qualification spec](../spec/issuer-qualification-v1.md), [program trust contract](../spec/program-v1.md), [annual accounting](../spec/annual-series-accounting.md).
4. **Mainnet release controls (engineering/security/operations).** Commission independent program, SDK, frontend, signer and infrastructure review appropriate for real assets; address findings and prove the exact audited build/IDL, upgrade authority, admin and attestor policy, key recovery/rotation, incident stops, custody monitoring, RPC/provider resilience, dependency upgrades, load and installed-wallet behavior. Today Config administration is claimed by the verified program upgrade authority; admin can register assets and disable new admission but has no arbitrary vault sweep; the asset attestor controls observations, event records, completeness attestation and prefinal abort; the upgrade authority can still change program code. v1 asset policy/attestor fields are immutable, and production rotation/recovery is not yet designed. Claim mint/freeze authority rules do not eliminate issuer-controlled collateral powers. Current hosted controls, quotas and rollback are for a finite test service; the fifteen-profile observation fee reservation would last at most 16⅔ days from an unused 0.01 devnet-SOL cap at its current cadence. [Program contract](../spec/program-v1.md), [hosting operations](../docs/hosting-operations.md), [hosting dependency review](hosting-dependency-review.md), [catalog release](devnet-catalog-release-2026-09-20.md).
5. **Commercial and professional review (external).** Confirm issuer product and transfer terms, operational access to durable action data, user geography and distribution constraints, disclosures for stock-paid PT/DR risks, and the legal/regulatory treatment of the proposed claim and market flow with qualified counsel. Seek a real liquidity counterpart and venue/market operating plan; the Raydium test pool establishes technical interoperability, not demand, spreads, usable liquidity, permission from an issuer, or a partnership. These are dependencies to assess, not conclusions that the product is lawful or unlawful in any jurisdiction. [Issuer synthesis](solana-issuer-synthesis.md), [issuer data requests](issuer-data-requests.md), [AMM review](amm-review.md).

## Annual lifecycle that any claim must preserve

For a 2027 series, deposits close **at** `2027-01-01T00:00:00Z` (exclusive); there is no midyear deposit into that series. Official reference-market civil ex-dates in 2027 define eligible events. Maturity at `2028-01-01T00:00:00Z` closes the date window but **does not itself finalize** a journal or unlock independent redemption. A late-paid 2027-ex-date dividend still belongs to 2027; unresolved or unsupported records block unsafe finalization. Before finalization, an owner can recombine matching PT and DR one-for-one, including after accrual or maturity, if custody is healthy. After a complete, resolved journal is attested and finalized, PT and DR redeem separately for raw stock-token allocations. Bearer DR retains its accumulated entitlement without an expiry or forfeiture; zero-value DR closure needs explicit consent. Neither side promises a cash amount or dollar principal. [Annual accounting](../spec/annual-series-accounting.md), [annual product](../spec/annual-product.md).
