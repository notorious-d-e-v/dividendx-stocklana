# Solana yield-market prior art: Exponent and RateX

Checked 16 September 2026 against primary documentation and public source where available. This is a bounded architecture review, not evidence that either protocol supports tokenized stocks, Token-2022 Scaled UI Amount collateral, or DividendX's issuers. Recommendations are advisory; Astra owns the decision.

## Decision relevance

**Use both protocols as design precedent, but do not integrate or reuse either for the hackathon path.** Exponent is the stronger implementation reference because its core Solana program, Anchor IDL and generated types are public. Its Standardized Yield (SY) boundary supports DividendX's decision to normalize heterogeneous collateral before splitting claims. RateX supports the same high-level lesson through its Standard Token (ST), but its public materials describe a synthetic margin system rather than a simple fully backed splitter, and no public program source, IDL or SDK was found.

Neither reduces near-term implementation risk. Both are designed for continuously accruing yield over a term, specialized rate markets and recurring yield ownership. DividendX instead freezes one classified corporate action, allocates raw collateral once, and lets classic-SPL PT and DR redeem independent fixed pools. Adapting either protocol would add a wrapper/interface program, protocol governance and integration assumptions while leaving the hard stock-specific work—Token-2022 custody, issuer controls, event classification and factor arithmetic—unsolved.

| Question | Exponent evidence | RateX evidence | DividendX implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normalization | A vault binds one SY program and mint; separate interface programs wrap specific yield sources. | ST rebases quantity so one ST stays aligned with a quote asset across rebase, appreciation and distribution models. | **Adopt the boundary, not the wrapper:** keep internal normalized shares and issuer readers. Do not introduce a circulating SY/ST token until an external consumer requires it. |
| Split instrument | Deposited SY mints equal PT and YT; paired merge is available while active. | ST/YT are synthetic positions backed by YBA deposits or trader margin; PT is synthesized from ST and a short YT position. | **Keep fully backed paired PT/DR.** RateX's margin, liquidation and synthetic issuance are outside scope. |
| Ownership of yield | Transferable YT earns nothing merely by sitting in a wallet; it must be escrowed into an owner-linked `YieldTokenPosition`, which stages SY interest and emissions. | Docs say actual yield is distributed each calculation interval in ST and positions are recalibrated; public implementation details are unavailable. | **Do not copy continuous owner accrual.** A settled DR is a bearer claim on a frozen pool, so token ownership at burn is enough; this is simpler and AMM-compatible. |
| End of term | PT can redeem after maturity without YT; YT stops accruing and unclaimed yield remains collectible. | YT trends to zero at expiry; PT converts 1:1 to ST at maturity according to the product docs. | DividendX differs materially: both PT and DR remain redeemable after settlement for separate stock-token pools. Use explicit lifecycle states, not generic “maturity” semantics. |
| Adverse yield | Source enters emergency mode when the current SY rate is below its all-time high and blocks several operations; exact loss allocation is more involved than the product summary. | Margin losses use collateral-ratio liquidation, insurance and auto-deleveraging; that is position solvency, not proof of stock corporate-action handling. | **Keep `M1 >= M0`.** A zero-dividend event may follow explicit cancellation/recombination rules; a negative or unexplained observation blocks settlement without destroying claims. Specify recovery separately rather than importing financial-engine machinery. |
| Market | Rate CLMM holds PT/SY, quotes implied-APY ranges and routes YT through atomic strip/merge flash swaps. | Custom YT/ST concentrated AMM periodically adjusts balances for time decay; docs also describe leverage and an order book. | **Keep the chosen Raydium DR/test-money CPMM proof.** DividendX has no continuous implied-rate curve and does not need flash issuance or a custom AMM. |

## Exponent: useful source-level precedent, poor direct fit

**Evidence.** Exponent describes a fixed-maturity identity of one underlying producing one PT plus one YT, paired pre-maturity merging, post-maturity PT redemption and claimable residual YT yield ([yield stripping docs](https://docs.exponent.finance/user-documentation/yield-stripping-swap)). The source links below were inspected from moving `main` on 16 September 2026, not from a pinned checkout. The [public core repository](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core) README then recorded deployed commit `7011d1b46b542611543f8ed21836b165f2ab75ac`, onchain program hash `e584d8035cbe411f6dc3a24bdcd32a29e4bf953569c1c4971b23c03793a16b3c` and a mainnet address. The organization showed a source update on 18 August 2026, establishing publicly maintained source; the recorded deployment and current operational activity were not independently verified. The [vault state](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/programs/exponent_core/src/state/vault.rs) binds `sy_program`, SY/PT/YT mints, maturity, exchange-rate snapshots, PT backing, accrued yield and operation flags. The [strip handler](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/programs/exponent_core/src/instructions/vault/strip.rs) converts SY through its exchange rate and mints equal PT/YT; the [merge handler](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/programs/exponent_core/src/instructions/vault/merge.rs) burns paired claims while active and only PT after expiry.

Exponent's owner-accrual solution is explicit: its [YT deposit handler](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/programs/exponent_core/src/instructions/vault/deposit_yt.rs) says wallet-held YT does not actively earn; escrowed YT is recorded in an owner-linked position. The [position accounting](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/programs/exponent_core/src/state/yield_token_position.rs) uses exchange-rate/index deltas to stage SY and emissions. That avoids asking transfer hooks to move accrued yield between owners, but adds deposit/withdraw/claim transactions and position state.

The inspected PT/YT handlers constrain the token program to the original SPL Token Program even though they use shared token-interface account types. Nothing inspected establishes support for Scaled UI collateral or stock-token extensions. Exponent's docs list separate generic, Kamino, marginfi and Jito interface programs ([programs and security](https://docs.exponent.finance/user-documentation/security)); that validates an adapter pattern, not compatibility with a DividendX mint. The public repository includes the [core Anchor IDL](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/target/idl/exponent_core.json) and [generated TypeScript type](https://github.com/exponent-finance/exponent-core/blob/main/target/types/exponent_core.ts), but no public official client SDK or source for the separately deployed Rate CLMM was found in Exponent's public organization repositories.

**Inference.** A stock integration would require a new SY interface that safely custody-checks the exact Token-2022 mint and translates Scaled UI factor changes into Exponent's continuous exchange-rate model. That translation would blur DividendX's classified, one-event entitlement boundary and would still need an issuer event reader. Exponent also controls market configuration through mutable parameters and multisig, and source exposes per-vault enable flags and guardrail limits. User actions may be open on an admitted vault, but no permissionless new-market path was documented.

**Reuse constraint.** The repository states Business Source License 1.1 for the core program, while its root `package.json` says ISC. Review the license scope for the specific files and intended use before copying code. Direct program integration also needs Exponent confirmation of market admission, stable client surfaces, collateral extensions and stock eligibility.

## RateX: product precedent with material opacity

**Evidence.** Current RateX docs call the product a multichain margin and spot yield-trading protocol ([overview](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex)). Its [ST specification](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex/ratex-protocol/basic-concepts-of-yield-trading/st-standard-token) normalizes heterogeneous yield-bearing assets by changing token quantity while holding unit value against the quote asset. Its [minting design](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex/ratex-protocol/mechanism-of-leveraged-yield-trading/minting-of-st-and-yt) is synthetic: LP collateral or trader margin creates ST/YT positions, sometimes allowing YT beyond deposited YBA, with obligations managed by a liquidation engine. [PT](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex/ratex-protocol/mechanism-of-leveraged-yield-trading/earn-fixed-yield) is a composed ST/short-YT position rather than the simple paired mint used by DividendX.

The [RateX AMM](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex/ratex-protocol/mechanism-of-leveraged-yield-trading/ratex-amm) is a concentrated YT/ST market with interval-based time-decay adjustment. That is valuable evidence that generic constant-product spot pricing is not ideal for long-lived yield derivatives. It does not invalidate DividendX's bounded CPMM demo: DR belongs to one admitted, identified event and can trade before its final observed outcome is accepted; the quote is disclosed test money, and the goal is portability rather than production price efficiency.

RateX's public GitHub organization exposes audit reports and media assets, but no Solana source, IDL, SDK or license for the protocol implementation ([organization](https://github.com/RateX-Protocol), [audit repository](https://github.com/RateX-Protocol/Audit-Report)). The [contract-address page](https://docs.rate-x.io/ratex/getting-started/contracts-addresses) lists Solana programs and markets, but the displayed markets mature in 2025 and the page says it was last updated one year ago. The site and newer tokenomics remain accessible in 2026, yet those historical addresses do not establish current market activity or current interfaces. The docs do not prove that ST/YT are freely transferable SPL mints, explain wallet transfer accrual at program level, document permissionless yield-market creation, or show Token-2022 Scaled UI stock support.

## Recommendations and verification gates

- **Adopt now:** one collateral/event per series; normalization behind a narrow interface; equal paired issuance; pre-settlement paired recombination; explicit maturity/settlement state; status flags and per-operation limits; source-level conservation and adverse-rate tests.
- **Keep the existing DividendX design:** internal shares, raw one-event allocation, classic SPL PT/DR, bearer redemption against frozen independent pools, and a general Raydium CPMM later. Exponent and RateX do not justify continuous accrual positions, a third circulating wrapper, leverage, liquidation, a custom rate AMM or synthetic claims.
- **Defer integration:** Exponent market routing and RateX market access until after the vault and external AMM proof. Neither is a lower-risk substitute for that proof.
- **Verify before any later reuse:** exact deployed source/IDL hash and upgrade authority; license scope; supported token programs/extensions; permissionless user access versus governed market admission; API/SDK stability; custody of paused/frozen/delegated stock tokens; whether a venue will list securities-derived claims; expiry and recovery behavior; and whether AMM-held claims can collect/redeem without owner-specific staging.

No additional Solana project was included merely for using “RWA”, “stocks” or “yield” language. Direct evidence found in this pass did not show another implemented Solana stock-dividend stripping system with enough technical detail to improve the decision.
