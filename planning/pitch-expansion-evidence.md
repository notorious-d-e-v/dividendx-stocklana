## Recommended roadmap copy

> **KOx first. Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA next. Then Backpack and Ondo on Solana—and, over time, Coinbase and Robinhood assets from other networks—with Solana as DivX's home base for global onchain dividend liquidity.**

This is intentionally aspirational. It says where DivX intends to go without claiming integrations, partnerships or universal bridging.

## Technical notes behind the slide

- **Solana representation today:** xStocks, selected Backpack-issued tokens, and Ondo Stocks. **Other-chain roadmap:** Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base and the new Robinhood Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain.
- Do not say every tokenized stock can simply bridge to Solana. Exact routes, custody, finality, token controls, eligibility and corporate-action evidence must be verified per issuer and asset. Cross-chain dividend claims may require locked collateral plus messaging, or a deployment on the collateral's own chain.
- Do not describe all Robinhood stock tokens as nontransferable. The restriction applies to the legacy Classic product; the new Robinhood Chain product is a different, wallet-held instrument.
- Do not use Backpack's brokerage catalog as a token-availability list. As of the snapshot, MU and SKHY were withdrawable; Backpack AAPL and NVDA were not.
- Do not imply an Ondo GM bridge route to Solana merely because Ondo Stocks are issued natively on Solana. Its current bridge documentation omits Solana for GM tokens.
